# Open Logic in Arabic — Sets chapter EPUBs

These two reflowable EPUB 3.3 readers contain the project introduction and the
complete Sets chapter: extensionality; subsets and powersets; important sets;
unions and intersections; pairs and products; and Russell's paradox. Their
source scope is eight selected units out of 722: OLP-0001, routing-only OLP-0004,
and all six sections OLP-0005–OLP-0010. The other 714 units are excluded.
This is a chapter edition, not a complete-book EPUB. No Classical EPUB is included.

Both readers have Arabic language metadata, RTL prose and page progression,
LTR native MathML, exact TeX formula annotations, seven navigation destinations,
nine XHTML spine entries, and three SVG diagrams converted from the source paths.
The international profile displays mathematical digits 0–9. The Machrek profile
displays mathematical digits ٠–٩; formula order, prose and mathematical meaning
are shared. Open Logic Project is the credited source creator. The upstream
source commit is 9620cc73f9c8e0ad003c514a5d3748f29611c4c0.

The accepted 2026-09-10 candidate passed 15 focused tests, EPUBCheck 5.3.0 with
zero messages for each EPUB, and 5,423 structural/source/link checks. All 327
source mathematical occurrences per profile have exact source crosswalks.
Independent content checks cover the six complete sections, including proofs,
exercises, annotations and the disposition of corrected identifier issues.
Both EPUBs matched independent rebuilds byte for byte. Existing epub.js/Chromium
checks loaded the actual archives; ten representative images were inspected.
These checks do not certify the entire translation-choice audit, all reading
systems, human-tested accessibility, or completion of the remaining 714 units.

The companion source/evidence ZIP contains the eight frozen Arabic sources,
matching eight upstream English source files, required configuration and diagram
definitions, converter and tests, finite English grammar dependencies, original
licences/notices, source freeze, QA reports and formula/source crosswalks.
EDITORIAL_CHOICES.json records presentation, navigation, numbering, diagrams and
standalone conditional choices. All choices remain open to correction; later
expert evidence can improve the edition without being a prerequisite for release.
