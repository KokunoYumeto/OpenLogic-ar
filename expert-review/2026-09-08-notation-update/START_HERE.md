# Arabic translation review — start here

[Start with 30 selected translation choices](../2026-09-08-integrated-review/EXPERT_REVIEW_START_HERE.md). Each entry gives the English term or construction, the Arabic wording, the location where established, the reason for the choice and what a reviewer might want to double-check.

[Open the full recorded list](FULL_LIST.md): 1,089 human-readable decision entries, with the latest notation findings alongside them. For feedback, quote the decision ID or source location and suggest a correction; references to Arabic mathematical scholarship are particularly useful. All provisional choices remain open to correction.

The [new notation supplement](source-snapshot/repo/evidence/classical/TYPED_NOTATION_REPAIRS_20260908.md) explains corrections to subscripts, font sizes and graph-source conversion, as well as two unresolved RTL rendering problems. These are printing decisions, not new Arabic wording decisions.

This is ready to share as a **working review register**, not a finished audit. The translation register still has 533 unresolved lexical locations, 110 flags without explicit review questions, 33 missing English senses and 273 retrospective template-style rationales. Locations are linked source lines where verified, not certified final-book pages.
