- **Global priority rank / score:** #535 / 62
- **Sense / المعنى:** Propagate 'truth of the necessity statement', not truth of any statement.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** This propagates a mathematical clarification already present in the Classical text and is verified by an explicit two-world model. An atomic p can differ between worlds even when the universal necessity operator is world-independent.
