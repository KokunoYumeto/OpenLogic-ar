- **Global priority rank / score:** #120 / 100
- **Sense / المعنى:** Extensionality gives uniqueness of a set with specified members, conditional on existence; it does not imply unrestricted comprehension.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** New independent assessment dated 2026-09-06; not a reconstruction of the original translator's deliberation. - **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** New independent assessment dated 2026-09-06; not a reconstruction of the original translator's deliberation. We replace هو القول with يقتضي and the explanatory أي with strengthening بل, retaining every existing formula, quantifier and later proof. This corrects an inherited source overstatement; it is not a claim that the English explicitly made the needed distinction.
