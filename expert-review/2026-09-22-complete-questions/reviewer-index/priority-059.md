## propositional connectives — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">روابط القضايا</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `repair-compact-0301-0400:0385-M1`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #573 / 56
- **Sense / المعنى:** Truth-functional propositional operators used to construct formulas, including unary negation.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): روابط القضايا is a grammatical construct phrase: روابط is the governing noun and القضايا specifies its domain. It matches the local list of negation, conjunction, disjunction, conditional and biconditional. Because negation is unary, the name cannot be interpreted narrowly as only joining two complete propositions; الروابط القضوية is a plausible adjectival alternative, not an attested preference established here.
