## Countable Choice — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">الاختيار المعدود</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `TERM-COUNTABLE-CHOICE`
- **Sense / المعنى:** choice restricted to countable families
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** The phrase is concise and parallels the edition’s use of «معدود» for countable. It could be read as a finite count unless that term is already stable.
- **Useful alternatives / البدائل المفيدة:** مسلّمة الاختيار القابل للعد — More explicit but heavier.; الاختيار العددي — Could suggest numerical choice rather than countability.
