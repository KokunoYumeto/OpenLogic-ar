- **ID / الرقم:** `semantic-propagation-20260907:AR-OLP-0321-FUNCTION-RELATION-20260907`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #436 / 67
- **Sense / المعنى:** Kleene normal form uses an output function U and a computation relation T; totality refers to evaluation/decision procedures, not to conflating their types.

MSA now propagates that distinction and makes termination refer separately to function evaluation and deciding the relation. This preserves partiality of f: its unbounded witness search can fail to terminate even though each individual T test is decidable.
