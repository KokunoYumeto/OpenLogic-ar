- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-1ed34b4e1fd67e76`
- **Sense / المعنى:** Three distinct relation conditions: universal self-relation, universal absence of self-relation, and failure of universal self-relation.

On a nonempty domain, mixed self-related and non-self-related elements give a relation that is neither reflexive nor irreflexive. The Arabic explanatory sentence spells out this middle case, which supports keeping غير انعكاسية for simple negation. This is provisional and open to correction; it is not a reconstruction of the original translator's motivation.
