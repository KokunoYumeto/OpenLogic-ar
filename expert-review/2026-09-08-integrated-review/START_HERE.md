# Arabic translation review — start here

[Open the 30-choice review queue](EXPERT_REVIEW_START_HERE.md). It shows the English term, the Arabic wording we used, where it occurs, why it was chosen and what you might want to double-check. Follow a term for the longer explanation and further locations.

[Open the full recorded list](FULL_LIST.md) for all **1,089 human-readable entries**, including the latest corrections now integrated into one register. These are decision records, not 1,089 distinct words.

For feedback, quote the decision ID, the source location and your suggested wording or correction. References to relevant Arabic scholarship are especially useful. Every provisional choice remains open to correction.

This is presentable as a **working review register**, not a finished audit. Locations are linked source lines, not certified pages of the final book. There are still 533 unresolved lexical locations and 110 flagged entries without an explicit review question. Some reasons were reconstructed retrospectively; 273 entries still have template-style rationales. Missing evidence is not an attestation of established Arabic usage.

The complete Classical-style reader and exhaustive canon-grounded audit remain unfinished. Existing published reader PDFs are retained; they do not yet incorporate all the newer source corrections. [What this update contains and what remains](WORKING_STATE.md).
