# Current Arabic Open Logic work — 8 September 2026

This is a consolidated preservation update: the current integrated translation-review register, its linked source/evidence snapshot and the Eastern-Arabic/RTL development sources. It replaces neither an existing reader nor the historical review records.

## Included progress

- One integrated register: 1,089 human-facing entries, 1,173 raw records, 914 flagged choices and 2,919 occurrence groups. All 73 repair assessments were independently checked; the preceding 1,150 raw records and seven superseded proposals are preserved.
- The exact English, modern Arabic and Classical-style Arabic content sources for all 722 units: 2,166 source files. Source reconciliation is a structural/content check, not proof that all prose or terminology has been independently justified against Arabic scholarship.
- The current 722-unit Eastern-notation presentation and reversible notation ledger. The materializer's static check reports zero untreated candidates and exact inversion, including propagation of 18 updated canonical units. Its `release_eligible` field describes that static materialization check only; it is not whole-reader build approval.
- Updated RTL code, source checks and the private-cache probe receipt. The small probe's primary/replay byte identity and runtime checks passed. Those results do not certify the complete reader or every aspect of typography. The included evidence is a privacy-normalized preservation projection; original artifact hashes identify the originals, not silently rewritten runtime files.

## Not yet complete

The full Classical build stopped at the first sets chapter: a typed numeral immediately after a subscript produced `Missing { inserted` at presentation `sets-functions-relations/sets/basics.tex`, line 31. The build failed, its captured process tree ended, and no partial PDF is offered as a reader. The next concrete production step is to repair that math-field interaction, then build and check the complete book, replay it and bind the review list to final PDF pages.

The exhaustive canon-grounded audit, full-book typography/formula checks and final page mapping remain unfinished. The review register explicitly retains gaps and retrospective evidence; passing export checks does not turn these into established terminology attestations. Human corrections are welcome and can inform later revisions, but are not a gate on continued work.

The current update preserves source improvements online immediately. Later substantive completed improvements will be consolidated into a further GitHub/Zenodo version, not published after each diagnostic correction. Both existing published Arabic reader PDFs and all earlier public files remain available unchanged.
