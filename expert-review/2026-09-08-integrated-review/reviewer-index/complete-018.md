## designated truth value — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">قيمة صدق مميزة؛ مختصرًا قيمة مميزة</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `TERM-DESIGNATED-VALUE`
- **Sense / المعنى:** truth value counted as acceptable/true for satisfaction and consequence
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** The chosen form consistently marks membership in V+ without equating every designated value with the distinguished symbol True. That distinction is essential in LP, Hal and R-Mingle.
- **Useful alternatives / البدائل المفيدة:** قيمة معيّنة — possible established calque, but may be heard as merely specified; قيمة مقبولة — explanatory but weakens the technical link to V+
> ⚑ **PLEASE DOUBLE-CHECK THIS CHOICE / يُرجى التحقق من هذا الاختيار:** Which Arabic term is officially preferred for designated value, especially when more than one value lies in V+?
