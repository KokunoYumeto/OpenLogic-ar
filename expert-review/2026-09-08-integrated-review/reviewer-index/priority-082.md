## successor function; f-closed; closure under f; Dedekind algebra — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">دالة اللاحق؛ منغلقة تحت f؛ الإغلاق تحت f؛ جبر ديديكند</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-5f95a92ca4bf5348`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #805 / 20
- **Sense / المعنى:** The successor function generates the natural-number sequence from numerical 0. For an endofunction f:A→A, an f-closed subset X contains f(x) whenever it contains x. The closure of o∈A under f is the least such subset containing o. A Dedekind algebra requires o outside the image of f, injectivity of f, and A equal to this closure.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): retain دالة اللاحق for the map, منغلقة for the stability property, الإغلاق for the least generated set, and جبر ديديكند for the structure satisfying all three clauses. Their roles are different and should not collapse into one word for completeness or closure. The abstract basepoint is the Latin letter o, while the motivating natural-number example uses numeral 0. Both current Arabic registers explicitly include zero in the motivating least-closed-set condition and restrict the general closure construction to f:A→A and subsets of A, with a correction note. Those are visible mathematical clarifications beyond the printed English wording, not merely lexical substitutions or proof of exact formula identity. This assessment is provisional and open to correction, not reconstructed translator deliberation.
- **Assessment provenance / توثيق التقييم:** New assessment, not a claim about the original translator's reasoning. ## symmetric; anti-symmetric; asymmetric; not symmetric — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">متناظرة؛ مضادّة للتناظر؛ لا تناظرية؛ غير متناظرة</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-59ab2802831b97ea`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #807 / 20
- **Sense / المعنى:** Symmetry requires Rxy⇒Ryx for every x,y. Antisymmetry requires (Rxy and Ryx)⇒x=y; it permits, but does not require, reflexive pairs. Asymmetry prohibits Rxy and Ryx together for every pair, including x=y. 'Not symmetric' is merely the negation of symmetry and is a different condition.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): retain متناظرة, مضادّة للتناظر, لا تناظرية and غير متناظرة as four distinct labels because their defining quantifiers differ. The identity-relation example proves that symmetry and antisymmetry can coexist, and the last comparison distinguishes asymmetry from antisymmetry. The Arabic prose explicitly distinguishes مضادّة للتناظر from غير متناظرة; keeping لا تناظرية separate is likewise essential, not stylistic variation. These judgments are provisional and open to correction, not reconstructed original translator deliberations or an official terminology attestation.
- **Assessment provenance / توثيق التقييم:** New assessment, not a claim about the original translator's reasoning. [Original explanation and checked source witnesses / التعليل السابق وشواهد المصدر](../source-snapshot/repo/evidence/provenance/locale-ar/expert-review-amendments/questions-036-045-20260906.json).
