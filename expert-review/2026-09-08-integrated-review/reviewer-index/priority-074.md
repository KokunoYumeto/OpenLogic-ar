## Axiom of Choice in selecting a right inverse — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">معكوس أيمن؛ بديهية الاختيار</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-2f7f7686dd32e35d`

- **Sense / المعنى:** For a surjection f:A→B, a right inverse h:B→A chooses one preimage per y∈B and satisfies f(h(y))=y. Axiom of Choice supports the general simultaneous-selection assertion, while particular cases can be proved without it.

The exceptions matter: a natural-number domain supplies least preimages, a finite domain can be finitely ordered, and a bijection has unique preimages. The passage therefore does not claim that every particular inverse construction requires the axiom. The retained longer headword is explanatory; the two literal labels occur separately. Right follows the composition identity, not visual placement in RTL. This assessment is provisional and open to correction, not a reconstruction of the original translator's deliberation or an official dictionary attestation. The footnote explicitly exempts finite, natural-number and bijective cases; least or unique preimages explain those exceptions without silently strengthening the general choice-free assertion.
