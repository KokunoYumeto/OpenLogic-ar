## any Dedekind infinite set can be turned — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">وجود أي مجموعة لانهائية بحسب ديديكند يتيح إنشاء جبر</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `semantic-propagation-20260906:scope-0051-generated-carrier`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #498 / 67
- **Sense / المعنى:** Existence of a Dedekind-infinite set gives a singly generated Dedekind algebra whose carrier is a subset of it.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** The formal theorem asserts existence, and the proof takes A to be the closure of o under g inside D and restricts g to A. The old English/MSA lead-in instead suggests that every whole Dedekind-infinite carrier can be converted into such an algebra. Classical already expresses the existential consequence. The chosen MSA retains that reading and adds على مجموعة جزئية منها, identifying the carrier used by the proof. The theorem, both proofs, and the earlier C051-01 closure correction remain unchanged. This is a new source-based assessment, not a recovery of earlier translator intent.
- **New repair assessment / تقييم تصحيحي جديد:** 2026-09-06; not the original translator's deliberation. Previous MSA wording / اللفظ السابق: <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">كل مجموعة لانهائية بحسب ديديكند يمكن تحويلها</span>. [Dated rationale and source evidence / التعليل المؤرخ وشواهد المصدر](../source-snapshot/repo/evidence/classical/repairs/OLP0051_0060_0068_SCOPE_PROPAGATION_20260906.json).
