## assumption — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">فرض؛ افتراض</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `RETRO0051-0100-assumption`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #584 / 50
- **Sense / المعنى:** An open or discharged hypothesis at a leaf of a natural-deduction derivation, or an initial signed formula in a tableau.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** فرض is compact and Classical in running prose; افتراض is also inherited in rule labels and tableau explanations. The ledger exposes rather than hides the variation.
- **Useful alternatives / البدائل المفيدة:** مقدمة — not selected — Reserved for an inference premise and can obscure discharge status.; فرضية — possible alternative — Common but can mean hypothesis as a mathematical conjecture.
> ⚑ **PLEASE DOUBLE-CHECK THIS CHOICE / يُرجى التحقق من هذا الاختيار:** Should leaf assumption be standardized as فرض, افتراض, or فرضية, and should tableaux use the same word as natural deduction?
