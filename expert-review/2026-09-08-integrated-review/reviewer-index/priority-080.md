- **Sense / المعنى:** A partition is the family of disjoint nonempty blocks covering the underlying set. For the equivalence relation in this passage, the blocks are equivalence classes, while the quotient is the set of all those classes. One class is one block, not the whole partition.

- **Global priority rank / score:** #786 / 20
- **Sense / المعنى:** In this positive-integer example, a perfect number equals the sum of its positive divisors other than itself: 6=1+2+3. 'Perfect' names that arithmetic property, not a value judgment.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): retain تام because the sentence immediately gives the arithmetic criterion and the example 6. That explanation helps, but الفعلية is a separate wording worth scrutiny: its ordinary meaning need not convey exclusion of the number itself. The source shorthand also omits the word 'positive' before divisors; the explicit list 1,2,3 supplies that intended restriction. Regional-attestation claims in the retained ledger are not independently established by this passage.
