- **Global priority rank / score:** #405 / 80
- **Sense / المعنى:** An absolute intersection of a set-indexed family needs a nonempty family here; empty intersections require an explicitly fixed ambient universe.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** New independent assessment dated 2026-09-06; not a reconstruction of the original translator's deliberation.
