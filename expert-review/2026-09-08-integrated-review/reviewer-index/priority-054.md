## ordinal rank under the imperative calculate — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">احسب أولًا العدد المثلثي ذا الرتبة (n+m)</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `semantic-propagation-20260907:AR-OLP-0031-CLASSICAL-ARABIC-ORDINAL-20260907`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #527 / 62
- **Sense / المعنى:** Write g(n,m) for g((n,m)); take triangular number of index n+m and add n.
