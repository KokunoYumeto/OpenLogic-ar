## modular arithmetic; congruence modulo n — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">الحساب بترديد عدد</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-21d39aea9b39f333`
- **Sense / المعنى:** For a fixed positive modulus n, a≡ₙb means that division by n gives equal remainders, equivalently a−b=kn for an integer k. This is a congruence relation, not numerical equality; its residue classes are the objects subsequently counted.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): retain الحساب بترديد عدد as the actual Arabic name used in the example. The integer requirement on k allows negative differences: 1 and 4 are congruent modulo 3 with k=−1. The initial source wording groups a,b,n under PosInt, while the later quotient is over Nat and explicitly uses the class of 0. That is a shared domain-scope question worth making explicit, not evidence for inventing a dictionary-approved congruence label. This assessment is provisional and open to correction; it does not reconstruct the original translator's deliberation or claim official dictionary attestation.
