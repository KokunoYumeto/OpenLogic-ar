# Arabic translation review — full recorded list

This consolidated register contains **1,089 human-readable entries**, backed by 1,173 raw decision records. The latest 23 applied decisions are incorporated; seven superseded proposals remain visible as history, not additional current choices. All 73 recorded repair assessments passed independent readback.

- [Browse all entries in readable parts](reviewer-index/README.md). Use the **Complete shards** section for the complete list; **Priority shards** contain the 914 flagged choices.

Entries record the English term or construction, chosen Arabic, rationale, alternatives and uncertainty where supplied, together with exact source locations where verified. There are 2,919 occurrence groups; these are not counts of unique words or of fully audited pages.

The list is **not yet an exhaustive account of every translation choice**. It retains 533 unresolved lexical locations, 110 flags missing explicit questions, 33 missing English senses and 273 retrospective template rationales. No final PDF page mapping is certified. The 55,124 local-link checks reported for the original snapshot did not fetch its external URLs; this public projection separately checks the relocated local links.
