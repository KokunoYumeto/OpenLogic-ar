## Choices for expert double-checking

Two repaired choices remain especially useful for expert review. They do not
delay the edition, and both are reversible.

1. **Quantifier — OLP-0104, 0108, 0112, 0115, 0117, 0120, 0123, 0128.**
   Chosen: `المكمّم` / `المكمّمات`. Motivation: it names the formal binding
   operator and is the dominant term in the local corpus. Please double-check
   whether the intended historical register should instead prefer `السور`, or
   should reserve distinct uses for `السور` and `المكمّم`. `الكمّيات` was not
   retained because it is easily read as “quantities.”

2. **Arity — OLP-0177.** Chosen: `عدد المواضع` and `علاقة ذات k مواضع`.
   Motivation: this is descriptively exact and avoids collision with the
   section’s repeated use of `الرتبة` for first- and second-order logic. Please
   double-check whether an official mathematics dictionary establishes a more
   compact noun for arity that should be used corpus-wide. The forms
   `ذات الرتبة k` and `k-المحلية` were rejected here as locally ambiguous or
   opaque.

The machine-readable entries preserve alternatives, rationale, exact source
locations, current hashes, and `open_to_correction: true`; they should feed the
final human expert-review index rather than being lost in a prose summary.
