## Mathematical and linguistic reasons

- **0321-P1 — MSA-only propagation.** The same proof uses `T(e,x,s)` under an existential witness condition and defines T through a formula, whereas U supplies a numerical output. Calling both `الدالتان` conflates a relation with its characteristic function. The selected phrasing separately says that evaluating the function and deciding the relation terminate for each input. It does not say the partially computable f always terminates. Classical already distinguishes the two types and is not rewritten.
- **0326-P1 — shared first-order correction.** Take domain {1,2,3}, the P-extension {1}, and the R-extension {2}. The sets are mutually disjoint, but at 3 the assertion “P iff not R” is false. Thus mutual non-overlap alone is insufficient: each extension must be the complement of the other in the domain. `هو بالضبط` in MSA and `هو بعينه` in Classical make that exact identification explicit. Both register-specific phrasings are now corrected; neither is described as already correct before this batch.
- **0326-P2 — MSA-only second-order propagation.** The same counterexample applies to the assigned extensions s(X), s(Y). Classical already used `بالضبط`, and the equation following both glosses already states the correct complement. Both original plural element tokens, all assignment expressions and the equation are retained.
- **0368-P1 — MSA-only conditional uniqueness.** If P and Q are two normal forms reached from a term, confluence gives a common reduct. Since P and Q have no proper reduction, that reduct equals both. This proves uniqueness only if a normal form exists. Omega = (lambda x.x x)(lambda x.x x) has a single beta-redex and reduces back to itself, so it has no normal form. The new MSA clauses distinguish uniqueness from existence explicitly. Classical's introduction and both later conditional proof passages are retained.

Alternatives deliberately rejected or not selected are recorded separately for every decision: `الدالتان`, declaring the relation itself “total,” reciprocal non-overlap, an unexplained compact complement term, unqualified “one value,” and an assertion that every computation route terminates. No official dictionary lookup was performed; no universal absence or authority claim is made.
