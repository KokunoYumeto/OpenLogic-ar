# Full translation-choice register

[Return to the short introduction](START_HERE.md).

**[Open the browser-friendly index](reviewer-index/README.md)** to browse the complete list in small pages. The complete-list section includes all **1,095 human-readable choices**; the priority section selects those flagged for closer review. Priority is not a claim that the remaining choices have been proved correct.

For each choice, look for:

- The English expression and the chosen Arabic wording.
- The explanation, any recorded alternatives, and a specific question where one has been written.
- The linked source file and exact line where established, or an explicit notice that the location remains unresolved.

For searching or offline work, use the [occurrence table](EXPERT_REVIEW_OCCURRENCES.csv), [complete Markdown register](EXPERT_REVIEW_INDEX.md), or [compressed machine-readable register](EXPERT_REVIEW_INDEX.json.gz). The large Markdown file may be too long for GitHub's preview; the smaller pages above are intended for online reading.

The machine-readable register preserves **1,179 raw records**, including historical and non-human-index records. This is why its total differs from the human list. Counts are decisions, not a count of distinct Arabic words, translated pages, or every translation choice in the entire work.
