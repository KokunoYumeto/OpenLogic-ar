# Arabic Open Logic: translation choices for review

Working register, 20 September 2026. Suggestions are welcome; every choice remains open to correction.

**[Start with the selected review questions](EXPERT_REVIEW_START_HERE.md)** — a short, prioritized reading list.

**[Browse the full list](FULL_LIST.md)** — all 1,095 recorded human-readable choices, divided into manageable pages.

Each entry shows the English expression, our Arabic wording, why it was chosen, alternatives where recorded, and what might benefit from your advice. Follow the source links to read the surrounding English, modern Arabic and Classical-style Arabic text. A source-line location is not a claim about the page in an older PDF.

For feedback, please give the **decision ID**, the word or passage you would change, your proposed wording, and your reason or a published example. You can review one entry at a time; there is no need to read the whole register.

This update adds dictionary-backed explanations and comparisons for relation terminology, including antisymmetry versus asymmetry, connected relations, Cartesian products and equivalence. It also retains earlier source clarifications and their recorded reasons. Newly obtained evidence is labelled retrospective; it is not presented as the original translator's reasoning.

The register is still being completed. **243 entries retain generic explanations**, and **105 flagged choices still need a specific review question**. Some references still need precise location matching. These are remaining editorial tasks, not requests for experts to finish our basic documentation. See the [working state](WORKING_STATE.md).
