# What this working register does—and does not—establish

This snapshot presents **1,095 recorded choices** for Arabic Open Logic. It preserves the underlying 1,179 raw records and the English, modern Arabic and Classical-style source texts for all 722 source units, together with the current reversible Eastern-notation presentation sources. Text presence is not the same as completed linguistic verification.

The remaining documentation work includes:

- **243 generic rationales** that still need substantive individual explanations.
- **105 flagged choices** without a specific expert question.
- **530 lexical location records** whose exact match is unresolved. These are location references, not 530 untranslated passages or 530 distinct words.

There are 852 choices outside the generic-rationale category (77.8%). That is a rough documentation indicator, not proof that every one is sufficiently justified, that all choices have been identified, or that the translations are correct. Expert feedback is welcome now and is never a prerequisite for continuing editorial work.

Dictionary-backed assessments identify the actual consulted source, entry and page, distinguish exact attestation from related evidence, and state limitations. A modern mathematical dictionary does not by itself establish Classical Arabic prose style. New explanations are dated retrospective assessments; historical explanations are retained rather than silently overwritten.

The newest eleven assessments include the relation-specific contrast between antisymmetry and asymmetry, the distinct-element restriction in connected relations, the ordered-pair meaning of Cartesian product, and equivalence relative to a relation rather than identity. Their current alternatives are displayed separately from historical alternative records.

This publication updates the **review register and working source snapshot**, not the reader PDFs. The complete revised Classical reader and all three full EPUBs remain unfinished. Existing public PDFs, sources and the two short Sets EPUBs remain available; those excerpts must not be mistaken for the full EPUB deliverables.

Every public source link in the review pages points into this dated snapshot where applicable. Exact final PDF pages are supplied only where actual page evidence exists; otherwise the source locator or unresolved notice is shown.
