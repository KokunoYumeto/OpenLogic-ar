## scope-0051-generated-carrier — OLP-0051

A Dedekind algebra is constructed on the generated subset, not necessarily the whole original set.

[Exact current occurrence, source line 101](../../../source/locale/ar/content/sets-functions-relations/infinite/dedekind-algebra.tex#L101).

Before: كل مجموعة لانهائية بحسب ديديكند يمكن تحويلها.
Chosen: وجود أي مجموعة لانهائية بحسب ديديكند يتيح إنشاء جبر.

The formal theorem asserts existence, and the proof takes A to be the closure of o under g inside D and restricts g to A. The old English/MSA lead-in instead suggests that every whole Dedekind-infinite carrier can be converted into such an algebra. Classical already expresses the existential consequence. The chosen MSA retains that reading and adds على مجموعة جزئية منها, identifying the carrier used by the proof. The theorem, both proofs, and the earlier C051-01 closure correction remain unchanged. This is a new source-based assessment, not a recovery of earlier translator intent.

Sense: Existence of a Dedekind-infinite set gives a singly generated Dedekind algebra whose carrier is a subset of it.

المراجعة المطلوبة — مفتوح للتصحيح: هل توضح عبارة «على مجموعة جزئية منها» أن حامل الجبر هو المجموعة المولَّدة، لا المجموعة الأصلية كلها؟ وهل يمكن أن تُفهم «جزئية» هنا على أنها جزئية حقيقية بالضرورة، وهو غير مقصود؟

Uncertainty: The domain distinction is mathematically secure. The source lead-in may have intended informal shorthand, not an erroneous theorem. The chosen جزئية means subset, not necessarily proper subset.
Classification: inherited-source-expository-domain-overstatement.

Alternatives:

- وجود أي مجموعة لانهائية بحسب ديديكند يتيح إنشاء جبر ديديكند — retained-and-clarified: The existing Classical existential formulation is correct; adding the explicit subset makes the carrier distinction clearer in MSA.
- يمكن تحويل كل مجموعة لانهائية بحسب ديديكند إلى جبر ديديكند — rejected: Suggests a singly generated algebra on every entire Dedekind-infinite carrier, which fails for an uncountable carrier.

Mathematical reason / counterexample: For D=Nat×{0,1}, g(n,b)=(n+1,b) and o=(0,0), the closure is Nat×{0}, a proper subset. More decisively, every singleton-generated successor algebra is the orbit {f^n(o):n in Nat} and hence at most countable. D=P(Nat) is Dedekind infinite via the injective map X↦{n+1:n∈X}, which omits {0}; Cantor diagonalization makes D uncountable, so no such algebra has all of D as carrier.

## scope-0060-hypothesis-final-index — OLP-0060

The induction hypothesis uses the final index, not the number of sequence entries.

[Exact current occurrence, source line 124](../../../source/locale/ar/content/propositional-logic/syntax-and-semantics/formation-sequences.tex#L124).

Before: طولها.
Chosen: رقم حدّها الأخير.

The proof explicitly inducts on n for a sequence indexed from 0 through n. Calling the smaller parameter m a length mismatches that parameter by one. Classical already says رقم حدّها الأخير. Propagating this wording keeps every displayed bound and formula unchanged. The same repair is made separately at the later application to the two prefixes. The earlier general statement that actual sequence length can be used for induction is true and is preserved.

Sense: The sequence A_0,...,A_n has final index n and length n+1; the displayed strong-induction parameter is n.

المراجعة المطلوبة — مفتوح للتصحيح: هل تعني «رقم حدّها الأخير» بوضوح فهرس الحد الأخير، لا عدد الحدود؟ وهل «فهرس» أوضح من «رقم» في هذا الموضع مع بقاء الترميز كما هو؟

Uncertainty: High confidence in the off-by-one diagnosis. The preferred Arabic label for final index remains open to expert correction; no new induction principle or formula reindexing is claimed.
Classification: inherited-source-proof-index-length-off-by-one.

Alternatives:

- طول متتالية التكوين — rejected-for-this-parameter: A legitimate alternative induction measure only if all parameters and bounds are reindexed to actual lengths. That would needlessly change formulas here.
- فهرس حدّها الأخير — open-alternative: May express subscript more technically, but no exact official-dictionary attestation was checked; رقم follows the already coherent Classical reading.

Mathematical reason / counterexample: In the formation sequence <p,not p>, n=1. Its required prefix <p> has length 1, which is not less than n, but final index 0, which is less than n. Generally a prefix ending at j=n-1 has length n and final index n-1. The n=0 induction hypothesis is vacuous and the atomic case supplies the base.

## scope-0060-prefix-final-index — OLP-0060

The induction application uses the same final-index measure for both prefixes.

[Exact current occurrence, source line 140](../../../source/locale/ar/content/propositional-logic/syntax-and-semantics/formation-sequences.tex#L140).

Before: فطول كل منهما.
Chosen: فرقم الحد الأخير في كل منهما.

Changing only the induction hypothesis would leave this later application with the old false length bound. The definition gives j<n and k<n, precisely the smaller final indices needed. The revised phrase explicitly reuses the hypothesis parameter for both prefixes, while retaining every formula and the lemma reference.

Sense: The initial subsequence ending at j has final index j<n, even when its length j+1 equals n.

المراجعة المطلوبة — مفتوح للتصحيح: هل تُحيل عبارة «فرقم الحد الأخير في كل منهما» بوضوح إلى معامل الاستقراء نفسه في «رقم حدّها الأخير»؟ يرجى التحقق من عدم فهمها على أنها طول المقطع.

Uncertainty: High confidence in the off-by-one diagnosis. The preferred Arabic label for final index remains open to expert correction; no new induction principle or formula reindexing is claimed.
Classification: inherited-source-proof-index-length-off-by-one.

Alternatives:

- فطول كل منهما أقل من n — rejected: A prefix ending at n-1 has n entries. Retaining this phrase would leave the proof application incorrect after the hypothesis repair.
- ففهرس الحد الأخير في كل منهما — open-alternative: Could name the same index more technically; it should be coordinated with the hypothesis wording, not silently mixed.

## scope-0068-used-premise-membership — OLP-0068

Every used premise must belong to the allowed set; not every allowed premise must be used.

[Exact current occurrence, source line 45](../../../source/locale/ar/content/first-order-logic/proof-systems/axiomatic-deduction.tex#L45).

Before: وتُتخذ.
Chosen: وتكون كل.

The earlier rule (2) admits any sentence belonging to the given Γ. The old parenthetical instead equates Γ with exactly the assumptions used. That excludes legitimate derivation witnesses with unused allowed premises and is incompatible with infinite Γ and finite derivations. Classical already requires each used premise to belong to Γ and visibly explains the source correction. Both its parenthetical and its note are copied exactly. The inference rules, axioms, theoremhood clause and worked derivation remain unchanged.

Sense: The used-assumption set is a finite subset of Γ, which itself may be finite or infinite.

المراجعة المطلوبة — مفتوح للتصحيح: هل تميز الجملة الاعتراضية والتنبيه بين المقدمات المسموح بها والمقدمات المستعملة فعلًا، بما يشمل مجموعة مقدمات غير منتهية؟ وهل «جزءًا» واضح في معنى المجموعة الجزئية غير المشروطة بالحقيقية؟

Uncertainty: The equality-versus-membership mismatch is clear from rule (2). The finiteness statement follows the finite-derivation convention and existing Classical disclosure; this audit does not validate every proof-system chapter.
Classification: inherited-source-used-premises-equality-versus-membership.

Alternatives:

- تكون مجموعة الفروض المستعملة مساوية لمجموعة منتهية جزئية من Γ — not-selected: Mathematically valid, but each-used-premise membership directly matches rule (2) without introducing an unnecessary intermediate set.
- مجموعة المقدمات هي بالضبط مجموعة الفروض المستعملة — rejected: Conflates allowed premises and used premises and rules out finite derivations from an infinite premise set.

Mathematical reason / counterexample: For Γ={p_0,p_1,...}, the one-line derivation p_0 is justified by membership rule (2), but uses only {p_0}. No finite derivation can use every member of this infinite Γ. With Γ={p_0,p_1}, the same one-line witness still need not use p_1. This finite example distinguishes permissible witnesses, not whether another longer derivation could use every premise in a finite Γ.
