# OLP-0021: two roots, not two positive roots

Please check the MSA note at [function-basics.tex, lines 76–77](../../../source/locale/ar/content/sets-functions-relations/functions/function-basics.tex#L76).
This affects both International and Machrek; the already-correct Classical
note is unchanged. Final PDF pages are not yet bound.

- Previous: **الجذور الموجبة للعدد الصحيح الموجب**.
- Chosen: **بأن للعدد الصحيح الموجب جذرين**.
- Why: the preceding formulas give the two roots with opposite signs. For
  input one they are one and minus one, not two positive numbers. The
  positive-input qualification and principal/nonnegative-root correction
  remain intact. No formula or technical terminology macro was changed.
- Expert question: هل يبين هذا التصويب أن وصف «الموجب» للعدد لا للجذرين
  معًا، مع بقاء الفرق واضحًا بين الجذرين والجذر الرئيسي غير السالب؟
