## Intersection — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">التقاطع</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `retro-0005-0050:named-defn:f127e60a4c6019a6`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #359 / 82
- **Sense / المعنى:** Intersection of two arbitrary sets A and B: exactly the elements belonging to both; membership is the conjunction x∈A ∧ x∈B. It may be empty.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-09; open to correction): Retain التقاطع as the definition label. I first read the academy notation book, physical PDF p. 95 / printed p. 83, section (5), where إشارة التقاطع names ∩ and the adjacent explanation specifies العناصر المشتركة. The book's common-members sense matches EN OLP-0008 L71–79, not merely the ordinary geometric idea of two lines crossing. The MSA definition at L70–78 says membership in كل من A وB; the Classical definition at L65–73 says membership in A وB معًا. Each is immediately fixed by the unchanged conjunction in the set-builder formula, and each permits an empty intersection. The noun is therefore retained for a specific set-theoretic reason and actual target-language evidence. This consultation was retrospective; the notation book does not establish medieval usage or certify the English-Arabic dictionary mapping.
- **Assessment provenance / توثيق التقييم:** New assessment, not a claim about the original translator's reasoning.
