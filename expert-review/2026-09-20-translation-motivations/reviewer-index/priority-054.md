## U and T are primitive recursive and therefore total — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">U دالة وT علاقة عوديتان بدائيتان؛ ومن ثم ينتهي حساب الأولى والبت في الثانية لكل مدخل.</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `semantic-propagation-20260907:AR-OLP-0321-FUNCTION-RELATION-20260907`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #518 / 67
- **Sense / المعنى:** Kleene normal form uses an output function U and a computation relation T; totality refers to evaluation/decision procedures, not to conflating their types.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** The MSA noun الدالتان incorrectly explicitly types both objects as functions. T(e,x,s) occurs as a truth-valued witness condition under existential quantification, while U returns an output number. The Classical source already states the function/relation distinction. MSA now propagates that distinction and makes termination refer separately to function evaluation and deciding the relation. This preserves partiality of f: its unbounded witness search can fail to terminate even though each individual T test is decidable.
- **New repair assessment / تقييم تصحيحي جديد:** 2026-09-07; not the original translator's deliberation. Previous MSA wording / اللفظ السابق: <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">حيث الدالتان U وT عوديتان بدائيتان، ومن ثم كليتان.</span>. [Dated rationale and source evidence / التعليل المؤرخ وشواهد المصدر](../source-snapshot/repo/evidence/classical/repairs/OLP0321_0326_0368_SEMANTIC_SCOPE_20260907.json).
- **Literal source passage / اللفظ في موضعه:** msa; English: where U and T are primitive recursive and therefore total.; before / السابق: <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">حيث الدالتان U وT عوديتان بدائيتان، ومن ثم كليتان.</span>; after / المختار: <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">U دالة وT علاقة عوديتان بدائيتان؛ ومن ثم ينتهي حساب الأولى والبت في الثانية لكل مدخل.</span>. [Exact source / المصدر](../source-snapshot/repo/source/locale/ar/content/incompleteness/incompleteness-provability/tarski-thm.tex#L54). 
- **Useful alternatives / البدائل المفيدة:** الدالتان — Conflates T with its characteristic function without saying so.; العلاقة كلية — Could suggest a total relation or universal truth; evaluation and decision termination are the intended properties.; الحساب الخاص بكل منهما كلي — Already distinguishes the object types in the preceding Classical clause; the present explanation for retaining it is retrospective, not a recovered original author rationale.
