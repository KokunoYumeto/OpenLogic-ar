- **Sense / المعنى:** An absolute intersection of a set-indexed family needs a nonempty family here; empty intersections require an explicitly fixed ambient universe.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** New independent assessment dated 2026-09-06; not a reconstruction of the original translator's deliberation. Inherited-source qualification: this makes the mathematical qualification or logical scope explicit, rather than claiming only a lexical mistranslation. Previous MSA wording: مجموعة من المجموعات. We carry the nonempty condition and its explanatory note into MSA and restrict only the intersection abbreviation. Empty unions and all displayed formulas are preserved.
