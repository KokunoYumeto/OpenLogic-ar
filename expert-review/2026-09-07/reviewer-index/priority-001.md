- **Sense / المعنى:** Two-sided sequent Γ ⟹ Δ whose antecedent and succedent are finite, possibly empty, sequences of sentences; not an ordinary sequence.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): This new assessment provisionally retains تتابعية as a short name because the definition immediately distinguishes the two-sided expression from the two sequences it contains.
