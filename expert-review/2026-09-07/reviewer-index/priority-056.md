## reduct / expansion — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">مختزَل / توسيع</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-62c0c8a085b90bd0`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #551 / 52
- **Sense / المعنى:** For languages L⊆L′, the L-reduct of an L′-structure forgets interpretations of symbols absent from L. An L′-expansion of an L-structure supplies interpretations of the additional symbols. The domain is unchanged and interpretations of all L symbols agree.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): retain مختزَل for the structure obtained by forgetting additional symbol interpretations and توسيع for a structure with those interpretations supplied. The direction is fixed by L⊆L′ and by the equality-of-domains clause, not by reducing or enlarging the underlying set. The subsequent agreement clauses cover constants, functions and predicates separately. These definitions support the conceptual contrast but do not establish a preference over regional alternatives such as توسعة. This assessment remains provisional and open to correction, not reconstructed translator deliberation or official terminology attestation.
