## symmetric; anti-symmetric; asymmetric; not symmetric — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">متناظرة؛ مضادّة للتناظر؛ لا تناظرية؛ غير متناظرة</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-59ab2802831b97ea`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #772 / 20
- **Sense / المعنى:** Symmetry requires Rxy⇒Ryx for every x,y. Antisymmetry requires (Rxy and Ryx)⇒x=y; it permits, but does not require, reflexive pairs. Asymmetry prohibits Rxy and Ryx together for every pair, including x=y. 'Not symmetric' is merely the negation of symmetry and is a different condition.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): retain متناظرة, مضادّة للتناظر, لا تناظرية and غير متناظرة as four distinct labels because their defining quantifiers differ. The identity-relation example proves that symmetry and antisymmetry can coexist, and the last comparison distinguishes asymmetry from antisymmetry. The Arabic prose explicitly distinguishes مضادّة للتناظر from غير متناظرة; keeping لا تناظرية separate is likewise essential, not stylistic variation. These judgments are provisional and open to correction, not reconstructed original translator deliberations or an official terminology attestation.
- **Assessment provenance / توثيق التقييم:** New assessment, not a claim about the original translator's reasoning. [Original explanation and checked source witnesses / التعليل السابق وشواهد المصدر](../source-snapshot/repo/evidence/provenance/locale-ar/expert-review-amendments/questions-036-045-20260906.json).
