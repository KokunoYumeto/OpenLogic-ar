The text's ordered ring adds a total order compatible with addition and multiplication by nonnegative elements. Its ordered field additionally gives every nonzero element a multiplicative inverse; both current Arabic versions expressly require 0≠1.

Both MSA and Classical visibly add 0≠1, with an explanatory correction note: the printed English clauses allow the one-element ring. That is a separately identified mathematical correction, not merely a translation variant or evidence of exact formula identity. This assessment leaves تبديلية versus تبادلية and حقل versus regional alternatives open to expert correction and does not reconstruct the original translator's deliberation.
