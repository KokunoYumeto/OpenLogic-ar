## predicative / impredicative — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">حملي / غير حملي</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `TERM-PREDICATIVE-IMPREDICATIVE`
- **Sense / المعنى:** definitions or comprehension principles that do or do not quantify over a totality containing the defined object
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** «حملي» connects the contrast with predication and is used consistently in the chapter. It is short, but may be confused with categorical-predicate logic unless the impredicativity sense is explicit.
- **Useful alternatives / البدائل المفيدة:** تنبؤي / غير تنبؤي — A false-friend rendering of English predicative.; تعريف دائري المجال / غير دائري المجال — Descriptive but too cumbersome and not extensionally identical.
