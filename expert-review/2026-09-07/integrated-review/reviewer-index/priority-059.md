- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-28c0cdc8866263cb`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #574 / 52
- **Sense / المعنى:** A mathematical set is a collection treated as one object, determined by its members rather than their order or repetition. This noun can denote sets of worlds, formulas, numbers, functions or other objects; it is distinct from the English verb 'set', such as 'set up'.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): Retain مجموعة because the primary definition explicitly introduces membership, the empty set and extensionality, while the inspected later contexts use the noun across different kinds of members. Those local definitions do not prove the retained rationale's claim of universal cross-regional standardness. Nor does a bare set name imply the added operation and laws of an algebraic group, so زمرة should not be substituted indiscriminately. Inspection of all 37 retained English/MSA locator pairs found one genuine verb/noun mismatch (OLP-0609: set up) and two same-noun but different-passage bindings (OLP-0023 and OLP-0332). These are rejected locator claims, not accusations that the Arabic word مجموعة is mistranslated. Exact replacement witnesses are supplied; the original recorded fields are preserved. This assessment is provisional and open to correction; no original translator deliberation or official dictionary attestation is claimed. Independent admission readback (2026-09-06): Every one of the 37 retained English/MSA pairs was reread. Exactly one pairs the English verb set up with an unrelated Arabic noun, and two pair different mathematical paragraphs. The other 34 establish this noun sense only; they do not certify the surrounding theorem or all Classical occurrences. Preserve the three retired records and use the exact replacement passages.
