## perfect number — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">تامًا؛ التام؛ قواسمه الفعلية</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-5f715e332c6c0516`
- **Global priority rank / score:** #774 / 20
- **Sense / المعنى:** In this positive-integer example, a perfect number equals the sum of its positive divisors other than itself: 6=1+2+3. 'Perfect' names that arithmetic property, not a value judgment.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): retain تام because the sentence immediately gives the arithmetic criterion and the example 6. Both Arabic registers use قواسمه الفعلية for 'proper divisors' and explain that these divide the number without remainder and are not the number itself. That explanation helps, but الفعلية is a separate wording worth scrutiny: its ordinary meaning need not convey exclusion of the number itself. The source shorthand also omits the word 'positive' before divisors; the explicit list 1,2,3 supplies that intended restriction. Regional-attestation claims in the retained ledger are not independently established by this passage. This assessment remains provisional and open to correction, not reconstructed translator deliberation.
- **Assessment provenance / توثيق التقييم:** New assessment, not a claim about the original translator's reasoning.
