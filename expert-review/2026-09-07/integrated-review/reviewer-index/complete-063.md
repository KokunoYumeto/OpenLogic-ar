## successor function; f-closed; closure under f; Dedekind algebra — <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">دالة اللاحق؛ منغلقة تحت f؛ الإغلاق تحت f؛ جبر ديديكند</span>

- **ID / الرقم:** `locale-ar-chosen-5f95a92ca4bf5348`
- **Sense / المعنى:** The successor function generates the natural-number sequence from numerical 0. For an endofunction f:A→A, an f-closed subset X contains f(x) whenever it contains x. The closure of o∈A under f is the least such subset containing o. A Dedekind algebra requires o outside the image of f, injectivity of f, and A equal to this closure.
- **Why this choice / سبب الاختيار:** Current assessment (2026-09-06; open to correction): New independent assessment (2026-09-06): retain دالة اللاحق for the map, منغلقة for the stability property, الإغلاق for the least generated set, and جبر ديديكند for the structure satisfying all three clauses. Their roles are different and should not collapse into one word for completeness or closure. The abstract basepoint is the Latin letter o, while the motivating natural-number example uses numeral 0. Both current Arabic registers explicitly include zero in the motivating least-closed-set condition and restrict the general closure construction to f:A→A and subsets of A, with a correction note. Those are visible mathematical clarifications beyond the printed English wording, not merely lexical substitutions or proof of exact formula identity. This assessment is provisional and open to correction, not reconstructed translator deliberation.
