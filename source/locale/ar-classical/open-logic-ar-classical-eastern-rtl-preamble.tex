% one mapping absent from the generic glyph list, at all three normal math
% sizes, so copy/extraction produces the meaningful `|→` composite.

% extensible constructs.  Although Open Logic selects the `ntxsyc` LaTeX font

% that backend name.  Multi-scalar mappings describe the two visibly composite
% square-arrow faces without inventing a Unicode character.

  glyphs={strict,strictinverse,squareright,squareleft},
